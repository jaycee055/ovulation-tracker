# Bloom — Ovulation Tracker

A clean, private ovulation and period tracker that runs in your browser and installs to your phone's home screen like an app.

**Open it:** https://jaycee055.github.io/ovulation-tracker/

## Features
- Cycle dial showing today's cycle day, fertile window and ovulation
- Pregnancy chance for today (low / medium / high / peak)
- Predicted next period, fertile window and ovulation day
- Calendar with logged and predicted periods, fertile days and ovulation
- Log flow, ovulation (LH) tests, symptoms, discharge, mood, basal temperature, intimacy and notes
- A positive ovulation test adjusts that cycle's ovulation estimate
- Learns your average cycle and period length from your history
- Insights: averages, recent cycles, how regular you are, top symptoms
- Back up to a file and restore
- Works offline; light and dark mode

## Add to your home screen
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome → tap **Add** in the app (or menu ⋮ → **Install app**)

## Privacy
All data stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking. See [privacy.html](privacy.html).

## How predictions work
Ovulation is estimated at 14 days (adjustable) before the next expected period, or the day after a positive LH test. The fertile window is the 5 days before ovulation, ovulation day, and the day after.

**Bloom is not medical advice and must not be used as birth control.**
