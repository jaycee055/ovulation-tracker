Good to know
Bloom estimates ovulation as 14 days before your next period (or the day after a positive ovulation test) and marks the 5 days before plus the day after as your fertile window. Cycles vary, so treat these as estimates — don't rely on Bloom for birth control. Talk to a doctor if your cycles are very irregular, very painful, or you're trying to conceive for a year without success.
