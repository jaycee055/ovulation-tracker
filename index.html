Good to know

Bloom estimates ovulation as 14 days before your next period (or the day after a positive ovulation test), and your fertile window as the 5 days before ovulation plus the day after. Cycles vary, so these are estimates — don't use Bloom as birth control. See a doctor if your cycles are very irregular or painful, or if you've been trying to conceive for a year.
