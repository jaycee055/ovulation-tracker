Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Bloom is a free ovulation and period tracker. It is built so that your health information stays with you.

What Bloom stores

The dates and details you log (period flow, ovulation tests, symptoms, discharge, mood, temperature, intimacy and notes) and your cycle settings. These are saved only in your browser's storage on your own device.

What Bloom does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no servers that receive your data.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your logs are never sent anywhere. We cannot see them.

What is loaded from the internet

The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services may see your IP address and basic browser information when the app's files are downloaded. They never receive your logs. After the first visit, Bloom works offline.

Backups

If you use Export, a backup file is saved where you choose. Keep it somewhere safe — anyone with the file can read your logs.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase everything, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Uninstalling the home-screen app may not remove browser data on every device, so erase first if you want to be sure.

Health disclaimer

Bloom's predictions are estimates based on averages. They are not medical advice and must not be used as birth control.

Children

Bloom is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Contact
